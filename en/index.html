About BergBuilds

Who's behind it?

One person, no office, and one rule: build it, show it, learn from it.

What is BergBuilds?

BergBuilds is one person's workshop in Denmark, building apps, mobile games, 3D asset packs and websites for small businesses. Everything is built in public and shown in short devlogs.

How does BergBuilds use AI?

As a partner: AI helps with code, writing, voices and 3D rendering, so one person can build a lot. Everything is tested and checked before it goes out.

Where can I get Lofye?

Lofye is on Google Play. Reppr and Nibblr are coming later.
