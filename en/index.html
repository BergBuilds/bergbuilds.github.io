FAQ

About BergBuilds

What is BergBuilds?

BergBuilds is a small Danish studio on Funen that builds apps, mobile games, 3D asset packs and websites for small businesses. Everything is built in public and shown in short devlogs.

Where is BergBuilds based?

On Funen in Denmark, near Odense. Websites are made for local businesses and remotely; the apps and asset packs are sold worldwide.

How does BergBuilds use AI?

As a partner: AI helps with code, writing, voices and 3D rendering, so one small studio can build a lot. Everything is tested and checked before it goes out.

Where can I get Lofye?

Lofye is on Google Play. Reppr and Nibblr are coming later.

Can BergBuilds build a website for my business?

Yes. See websites and prices, or get in touch.
