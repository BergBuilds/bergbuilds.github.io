FAQ

What people usually ask

What does it cost to keep the site running?

With a plan, hosting, backup and small changes are in the monthly price. The domain (about €15 a year) is in your name. If you buy the site, you only pay for the domain.

I'm not in Denmark. Does that matter?

No. Everything happens by email and video calls, and the site is written in your language. I invoice in euros.

Can I edit the text myself?

Yes, if you want to. Otherwise just send a message and I'll change it.

I already have a site. Can you make it better?

Yes. Often a new, simple site is faster and cheaper than patching an old one.

What about photos?

Your own photos work best. Otherwise we use free, high-quality stock photos.
