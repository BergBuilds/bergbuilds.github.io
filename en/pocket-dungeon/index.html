Turn-based roguelike

About the project

Monsters move when you move. Every floor is built at random on a small grid, and the monsters show what they're about to do, so each step is a little puzzle.

There's a boss every 5 floors, classes like knight, rogue and barbarian, weapons to unlock, and a daily dungeon.

The art is rendered in 3D with Blender, and the same models were used for a small 3D version in Unity.

  • Random floors
  • Bosses, classes and weapons
  • Daily dungeon
  • 3D version in Unity
