About the project

An experiment, not a money machine. Strategies are tested on historical prices first, and the best one keeps running on a paper account to see if it holds up in real life.

It has given some of the funniest devlogs, like the day the bot thought it had lost 4.6% when it was really just missing one price.

Questions about Greed
