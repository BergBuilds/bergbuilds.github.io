Nibblr

Calories, macros and fasting

Nibblr is a calorie and macro tracker where you describe a meal in your own words and get it worked out. It's in development.

All projects
StatusIn development
PlatformAndroid
Built withReact Native, Expo, SQLite, AI

About the project

Describe the meal, AI works it out. "Two eggs, a slice of rye bread and a coffee with milk" becomes calories, protein, carbs and fat.

Your own library. Ingredients you use often are stored locally on the phone, so it gets faster every day.

There's a built-in fasting timer, and your real energy use is worked out from how your weight actually changes.

  • Meals in your own words
  • Local ingredient library
  • Fasting timer
  • Energy use from your weight
