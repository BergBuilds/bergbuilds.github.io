About the project

One score for the whole arc. Your habits become one consistency percentage for the day, the week and the whole run to New Year, with a glowing curve, the week in rings and a leaderboard of your habits.

A guide to what to do each day. 24 small daily habits in four groups (Mind, Body, Food and Discipline), like reading 10 pages, writing in a notebook for 5 minutes or drinking 2 litres of water. Every habit has a line on why it's worth doing. Start from a ready template like "Full Winter Arc", "Gym Arc", "Study Arc" or "Soft Arc", and add your own.

Kind to the bad days. A missed day lowers the percentage a little, but never wipes it, and you get credit for your comebacks. Consistency isn't perfection.

Workouts from Reppr and food from Nibblr can tick habits off automatically. Everything stays on the phone: no account and no data sent anywhere. Read the privacy policy.
