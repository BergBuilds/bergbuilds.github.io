Privacy policy for Arcmode

Arcmode collects no personal data. There is no account, no ads and no tracking, and everything you enter in the app stays on your phone.

Last updated 9 October 2026.

Who makes Arcmode

Arcmode is made by BergBuilds, a small studio on Funen, Denmark. For any question about privacy, write to bergbuilds@outlook.dk.

What the app stores, and where

Your habits, the days you've ticked off, your score, your settings and anything you import from Reppr and Nibblr are stored locally on your phone. None of it is sent to BergBuilds or anyone else. The app has no server, no account and no sign-in.

Data from Reppr and Nibblr

If you use Reppr or Nibblr on the same phone, they can open Arcmode with a link that carries a short summary, such as the date and length of a workout or the day's calories and protein. You can also pick a Nibblr backup file yourself and import it. Both happen on the phone only.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings. Nothing is sent over the internet to show them.

Sharing

Arcmode can make a picture of your arc for you to share. It is only shared if you tap share and choose where it goes. After that, the app you share to applies.

No ads, no tracking

Arcmode shows no ads and uses no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools that send data off your phone.

How to delete your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's app settings. Then everything is gone, because it only ever lived on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated here with a new date at the top.

Questions

Does Arcmode collect data about me?

No. The app collects no personal data, and nothing leaves your phone unless you share a picture yourself.

Do I need an account?

No. Arcmode has no account and no sign-in.
