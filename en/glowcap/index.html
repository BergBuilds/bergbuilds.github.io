Glowcap

Idle game about fungi

Glowcap is an idle game about a glowing fungal network under the forest floor. You grow threads through the soil, find water and nutrients and evolve your fungus. It's in development.

About the project

The map is the game. Tap the soil to grow a thread there. Rocks block, springs give water, and old bones are treasure.

Four seasons change the game: spring makes threads cheaper, summer brings growth, autumn gives spores, and winter is harsh.

When the fungus fruits, you start over on a new map with spores to spend in a skill tree: the genome.

  • Grow your own network
  • Four seasons
  • Water, sugar and minerals
  • The genome: a skill tree
