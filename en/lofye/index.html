Lofye

Lo-fi focus timer

Lofye is a calm focus timer for study and work, with lo-fi music, rain and cosy scenes. The first BergBuilds app, and it's out on Google Play.

Get it on Google Play
StatusLive on Google Play
PlatformAndroid
Built withReact Native, Expo, TypeScript

About the project

Press start and focus. Pick Pomodoro, a countdown or a stopwatch, and let the music play in the background, with rain or other sounds if you like.

Along the way you get streaks, stats and tags to see where the time goes, and new songs, sounds and scenes unlock the more you use it.

The app went through a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days before it came out on Google Play.

  • Pomodoro, countdown and stopwatch
  • Lo-fi music, rain and other sounds
  • Streaks, stats and tags
  • Scenes to unlock
