Zeta

Automatic video editing

Zeta is a video editor that cuts by itself from a script: captions, music, beats and graphics. It makes BergBuilds' devlogs and app ads, and it runs entirely on the PC.

About the project

Script in, video out. One line per thought. Zeta finds the best takes, cuts pauses and filler words, adds captions word by word and cuts on the music's beats.

The modern style used by the devlogs and ads has headlines whose words slide in one at a time, numbers that count up, ticking lists and a small talking character. The same style is used on this website.

  • Edits from a script
  • Captions word by word
  • Music and beats
  • AI voice that runs locally
