About the project

Every video is one story from the week's work, with something to take away: a bug, a number that surprised, or an idea that worked.

The videos are written by Iota and edited by Zeta, and none go out before they're approved.

Questions about Devlog

What is the devlog?

The devlog is short videos on YouTube and Instagram about everything being built: what worked, what broke and what it taught me. Watch the latest here.

Who makes the devlog?

Devlog is made by BergBuilds, a small Danish studio on Funen that builds apps, games, asset packs and websites.

What is the devlog made with?

The devlog is made with Iota and Zeta. BergBuilds builds everything in the open with AI as a partner, so tools and code from one project often end up in the next.
