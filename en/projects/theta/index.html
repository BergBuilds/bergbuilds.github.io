About the project

One page, your own numbers. Theta reads a bank CSV or numbers you type in, and shows where you stand and how small changes move the financial-independence date.

It's built for personal use and isn't shared as a product. It's here because it's one of the tools built along the way.

Questions about Theta

What is Theta?

Theta is a private dashboard for your own money: net worth, spending against budget, savings rate and when you could be financially independent. Numbers only, never advice, and nothing is uploaded.

Who made Theta?

Theta is made by BergBuilds, a small Danish studio on Funen that builds apps, games, asset packs and websites.
