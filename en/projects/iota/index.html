About the project

Notes in, videos out. Every story gets a score, and only the best become videos. Every project has its own colour, so you can tell what a video is about.

Every week the videos are written and rendered on Sunday, approved, and then scheduled to YouTube and Instagram. See the latest devlogs.

Questions about Iota
