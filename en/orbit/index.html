About the project

One finger, endless levels. Levels are built at random and get harder: swaying planets, pulsars that breathe, and black holes that bend your flight.

Levels come in worlds of 10, and every tenth is a boss, beaten by landing on its moons.

Perfect landings give slow motion, camera punch and music that builds the longer your streak.

  • Random levels
  • Worlds and bosses
  • Black holes and pulsars
  • Ships and trails to unlock

Questions about Orbit

What is Orbit?

Orbit is a one-tap neon space game: tap to launch your ship out of orbit and catch the next planet's ring. It's in development.

Who made Orbit?

Orbit is made by BergBuilds, a small Danish studio on Funen that builds apps, games, asset packs and websites.

What is Orbit built with?

Orbit is built with React Native, Skia. BergBuilds builds everything in the open with AI as a partner, so tools and code from one project often end up in the next.

Where can I get Orbit?

In development. Follow the devlog on YouTube or Instagram to hear when it's out.

Can I follow how Orbit is going?

Yes. BergBuilds shares progress, mistakes and lessons from its projects in short videos on YouTube (@BergBuilds) and Instagram (@bergbuildsdev). New projects show up on the front page as soon as they've started.
