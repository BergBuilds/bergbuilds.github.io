About the project

Log a set in seconds. Exercise, weight and reps, without digging through menus.

A coach in your pocket. Reppr looks at your last sessions and suggests the next step: more weight, more reps, or a reset when you've stalled. Every muscle gets its own level.

Reppr can pass your training on to the calorie app Nibblr, so food and training fit together.

  • Fast logging
  • Suggestions for your next session
  • A level for every muscle
  • Programs that grow with you
