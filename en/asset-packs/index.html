About the project

How they're made: every model is built and rendered in Blender through ArtKit, BergBuilds' own pipeline, with AI tools helping.

RPG Item Icons (100 icons, $4.99): weapons, potions and treasure. 3D UI Icons (63, $7.99): clay-style buttons and symbols. Sci-fi Space Kit (39, $4.99): ships and sci-fi parts. Cozy Food & Cooking (51, $4.99): fruit, veg, bakes and dishes.
